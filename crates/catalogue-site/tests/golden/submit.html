What happens next

  1. You open an issue. The form opens on GitHub.
  2. A maintainer approves it. A maintainer listed in data/maintainers.toml must approve the issue. Approval is the approved label on the issue. The bot accepts that label only from a listed maintainer.
  3. The bot opens a pull request. It holds the data change.
  4. Merging applies the change. The entry page shows it on the next build.

Public review

Every judgment is public: the issue, your login, the evidence and the approver appear on the entry page.

An ml-rust organization member never approves a judgment on an org-affiliated entry.

Read the rules before you file a judgment.
